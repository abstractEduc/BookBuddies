Bookbuddies
Share your favorite books & leave voice notes for friends!
Bookbuddies lets you easily exchange your reading adventures. Send book recommendations, track your reading-list, and leave voice notes for your friends—blending words and voices to grow your book-loving circle.
Privacy first—always.
All of your books, contacts, and voice notes are stored locally on your device.
Nothing is ever shared with Bookbuddies' developers, servers, or third parties.
Only you and your chosen friends can access your data.
Your reading history and personal notes always remain private.
